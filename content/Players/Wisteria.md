**Played by Magnolia**

She lost her name in the Red Raids.

Forsaken by her family, she was thrown into Lower Waterdeep as a refugee.

She screamed. She cried. She begged. The guards did not help her. Nobody did. Not until he found her.

Yasin Loran, despite having recently lost his mother and sister, took her under his wing. He calmed her, consoled her, befriended her. He became her brother, her family, more than her blood, who never even tried to find her. She could not forget them, but her new family quickly became far more important, especially when he helped her find a new name.

Wisteria.

Able to finally witness Lower Waterdeep herself, her eyes were opened to the horrendous conditions the people had to struggle through. The Red Raids only served to exasperate and emphasise the pre-existing circumstances.

When Yasin told her of the rebellion his father was starting, she immediately volunteered with him. They protested as loud as they could, they made food more accessible for families in need, they completed laborious tasks for those who were unable, and many other things, as long as they were physically able to.

Over the past 10 years, she has been through many things with Yasin. Together, they have laughed, cried, been hurt, helped people, stolen, and lost body parts, among other things. Ever since they met, Yasin has always been there for her. Even when she left Lower Waterdeep to attempt to make change her own way, he never gave up on her. She owes him her life many times over, as much as he tries to convince her otherwise.

Wisteria will forever be loyal to her brother.
