### Mannerisms: 

Trained from a young age as an assassin, her movements are light and controlled, even when she appears relaxed. Her words are carefully chosen to disarm or catch others off guard, often hidden behind casual banter. She carries herself with an ease that conceals constant awareness of her surroundings.

### Allegiance: 

Unknown

### Public Perception:

Most people see her as pleasant, a little foolish, and easy to underestimate. Those who spend more time around her may sense that the persona is deliberate, though few can say why. She is generally regarded as nonthreatening and oddly lucky.

### Relationships:

[[Florien|Florien:]] *Neutral*

Arispira never had the chance to grow particularly close to Florien during their time in Mere. Their interactions remained light and cordial, settling into an easy but distant friendliness rather than anything deeper.

[[Kerrick|Kerrick Lurgan:]] *Slightly Negative*

Kerrick’s reputation as a Dragon-Slayer has always set Arispira on edge. Though he never treated her poorly and their shared experiences were largely neutral, she found his presence unsettling, lingering with a quiet sense of unease whenever he was nearby.

[[Rona|Rona:]] *Neutral*

Much like with Florien, Arispira didn’t spend enough time with Rona during the Mere experience to form a strong bond. Their relationship remained polite and friendly, but distant, built more on circumstance than connection.

[[Tarvo|Tarvo:]] *Positive*

Tarvo was someone Arispira genuinely connected with, forged through long stretches together in Revellia and Mere. She came to see him as a trusted friend, and learning that they had known one another even before her amnesia, during the Red Raids no less, left her deeply shaken and curious about what she had lost.
